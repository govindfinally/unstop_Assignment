Global maritime supply chain resilience depends on multi-port routing flexibility and buffered safety stock near high-demand consumption clusters. Historical freight studies show that single-corridor container dependency exposes importers to cascading demurrage fees whenever customs clearance slows by more than forty-eight hours. Leading logistics operators mitigate disruption risk through dual-sourcing critical sub-assemblies, real-time vessel telemetry, and dynamic reorder triggers tied to port dwell indices rather than static monthly calendars.
